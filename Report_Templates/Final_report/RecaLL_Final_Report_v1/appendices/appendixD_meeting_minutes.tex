\chapter{Meeting Minutes / Progress Logs}\label{app:minutes}

All four members live in the university hall and met in person every week before the lab session to plan,
work together and check each other's work. Formal minutes of these meetings were not kept. \Cref{tab:meetings}
is therefore a progress log reconstructed from the commit history and the team's weekly meetings: one row per
increment, with the dates taken from \texttt{git log -{}-date=short} and a description of what the commits
delivered.
